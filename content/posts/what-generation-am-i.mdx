ColorFun helps parents, teachers, and creators generate printable coloring pages in seconds. If you want pages that are fun for kids and easy to print at home, this guide covers the full workflow.

## What ColorFun Is Best For

- Creating **kids coloring pages** from a short text prompt.
- Adjusting output for different age groups.
- Producing clean line-art pages that are easier to print.
- Building themed sets for classes, activities, and family use.

## Fast Workflow

1. Enter your prompt (example: `cute dinosaur in a park`).
2. Select age level and page layout.
3. Generate and review the preview.
4. Regenerate with small prompt changes if needed.
5. Download and print.

## Prompt Writing Tips

Good prompts are short, concrete, and age-aware.

### Better prompt examples

- `friendly unicorn with big stars, simple thick lines`
- `school bus in front of a classroom, clean outlines`
- `farm animals set: horse, sheep, chicken, easy shapes`

### Avoid in kids prompts

- Too many tiny background details.
- Complex perspective or crowded scenes.
- Unclear style words that reduce print readability.

## Age-Level Suggestions

- **Younger kids:** big shapes, thick outlines, fewer small areas.
- **Older kids:** richer details, more textures, layered scenes.

When results feel too complex, simplify the prompt first, then regenerate.

## Printing Tips

- Use white background + high-contrast black lines.
- Test one page before printing a full set.
- For classroom packs, keep one style across all pages.

## Reuse and Iteration

Use your creation history to quickly revisit previous ideas, adjust prompts, and build a consistent coloring-page collection over time.

## Final Checklist

Before sharing or printing, verify:

- Line art is clear and closed.
- Important objects are not cut off.
- Detail density matches the target age.
- The page looks clean in a real print preview.
