## 1. Scope

This Privacy Policy explains how **ColorFun** collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our website and related features.

## 2. Data We Collect

### A. Account Data

- Name, email address, avatar, and account ID.

### B. Generation Data

- Prompt text, selected options (such as age level and layout), optional reference images, and generated coloring page results.

### C. Usage Data

- Device/browser info, IP address, pages visited, feature usage, timestamps, and error logs.

### D. Payment Data (if paid plans are enabled)

- Billing and payment processing is handled by payment providers.
- We typically receive transaction status and subscription metadata, not full card details.

### E. Cookie and Local Storage Data

- Used for login sessions, preferences, security checks, and basic analytics.

## 3. How We Use Data

We use data to:

- Provide and maintain coloring-page generation features.
- Return downloadable printable outputs.
- Keep account history and support re-downloads.
- Detect abuse, fraud, and technical failures.
- Improve model quality, UX, and performance.
- Process payments and subscriptions when applicable.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## 4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We may share data with:

- Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers.
- Authentication, analytics, storage, support, and payment vendors.
- AI/API providers required to process generation requests.
- Regulators or law enforcement when legally required.

## 5. Public Content

If you actively choose to share generated content publicly (for example, in a public gallery), that content may be visible to other users.

## 6. Data Retention

We keep data for as long as needed to operate the service, meet legal requirements, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

## 7. Security

We apply reasonable technical and organizational safeguards. No online system is completely secure.

## 8. Children's Privacy

ColorFun is designed for coloring activities but is not intended for direct use by children under 13 without a parent/guardian.

## 9. Your Rights

Depending on your region, you may request access, correction, deletion, or restriction of your personal information.

## 10. Changes

We may update this policy. Updates take effect when posted unless otherwise stated.
