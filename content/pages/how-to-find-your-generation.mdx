## How It Works

Using **What Generation Am I** is simple:

1. **Select your birth year**.
2. **Optionally select your birth month** for boundary-year context.
3. **Click Analyze with AI**.
4. **Review your result**: generation label, explanation, and characteristics.

## What You Get

- **Generation classification** based on your birth year.
- **Boundary-year hints** when your year falls near two generations.
- **Clear explanation** of why your result was assigned.
- **Generation traits summary** to help you understand context.

## Tips for Better Results

- Add your birth month when possible.
- Compare results for nearby years if you are on a boundary.
- Read both the short label and full explanation before sharing.

## Notes

Results are informational and may vary slightly across research sources because generation year ranges are not always identical.
