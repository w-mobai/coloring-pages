## Quick Start

1. Open the generator page.
2. Enter a short prompt that describes the scene or character.
3. Choose age level and layout.
4. Generate, review, and regenerate if needed.
5. Download and print.

## What to Include in a Prompt

Use this simple structure:

- Subject: what should appear (`dinosaur`, `train`, `horse`).
- Style: line-art expectations (`clean outline`, `thick lines`).
- Complexity: target age (`for young kids`, `more detail for older kids`).

Example:
`friendly dragon in clouds, thick clean outlines, for young kids`

## Best Settings by Audience

- **Preschool / early learners:** large shapes, minimal background details.
- **Elementary kids:** moderate detail and stronger scene variety.
- **Mixed class use:** keep style consistent across a set.

## Printing Checklist

- Use black line art on white background.
- Check for broken or too-thin lines.
- Print one sample page before batch printing.

## Troubleshooting

- Output too busy: simplify prompt and reduce detail words.
- Output too plain: add one or two concrete objects.
- Subject missing: put the main subject at the beginning of the prompt.
